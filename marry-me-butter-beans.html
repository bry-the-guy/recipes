Marry Me Butter Beans

A metric, lower-sodium adaptation with silken tofu and nutritional yeast.

4 servings · about 25 minutes (estimated)

Adapted from Thee Burger Dude.

Ingredients

  • 480 g drained butter beans (about two 400–425 g cans), rinsed
  • 30 ml olive oil
  • 15 g garlic, minced (about 5 cloves)
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes, optional
  • 1–3 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 120–240 ml low-sodium vegetable broth
  • 120–240 g silken tofu, blended smooth
  • 10–15 g Dijon mustard
  • 15 g nutritional yeast (replaces Parmesan and includes the original yeast)
  • 35 g sun-dried tomatoes, chopped
  • 25 g spinach, chopped
  • Black pepper, to taste

Directions

  1. Warm oil in a deep skillet over medium heat. Stir garlic and optional chilli for 30 seconds, then herbs for another 30 seconds.
  2. Mix in 120 ml broth and 120 g tofu. Add mustard, yeast, tomatoes and spinach; simmer 2–3 minutes.
  3. Fold in beans gently and heat through. Thin with remaining broth, tofu or water as needed. Season with pepper; taste before adding salt.
  4. Serve with bread, rice or pasta. Refrigerate leftovers for up to 3 days.

Notes

Metric weights are approximate. This version uses rinsed beans, low-sodium broth and nutritional yeast instead of vegan Parmesan. Use water instead of salty can liquid to loosen the sauce. No salt is added automatically.

Estimated nutrition per serving: approximately 250 kcal, 12 g protein, 30 g carbohydrate, 9 g fat and 9 g fibre. Estimate assumes 480 g drained beans, 180 g tofu and 180 ml broth; excludes bread, rice or pasta. Brands and quantities change the totals.
