Stuffed Pepper & Black Bean Skillet

4 servings · about 40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 15 ml olive oil
  • 3 bell peppers, thinly sliced
  • ½ medium yellow onion, thinly sliced
  • 30 g tomato paste
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp onion powder
  • ½ tsp dried oregano
  • ¼ tsp chili powder, optional
  • 185 g uncooked long-grain white rice
  • 600 ml low-sodium vegetable broth
  • 1 can diced tomatoes, about 400 g
  • 1 can black beans, about 240 g drained, rinsed thoroughly
  • 110 g shredded cheddar cheese
  • Green onion, chopped, for serving
  • Fresh coriander, chopped, for serving
  • 1 avocado, chopped, optional

Directions

  1. Heat olive oil in a large deep skillet over medium heat. Add peppers and onion and cook until softened and lightly browned.
  2. Add tomato paste, garlic, smoked paprika, cumin, garlic powder, onion powder, oregano and optional chili powder. Cook about 1 minute until fragrant.
  3. Add rice, vegetable broth and diced tomatoes. Stir, cover and simmer gently for 15–18 minutes, or until the rice is tender.
  4. Stir in the rinsed black beans and half the cheese. Cook another 2–3 minutes. Taste before adding any salt.
  5. Top with remaining cheese. Cover until melted, or broil 2–3 minutes if the skillet is oven-safe.
  6. Finish with green onion, coriander and optional avocado.

Lower-sodium note: Low-sodium vegetable broth, thoroughly rinsed beans and individual spices replace the original taco seasoning. Taste before adding salt because the tomatoes and cheese contribute sodium.
