Mexican Veggie Mince & Cabbage Skillet

6 servings · about 40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 800 g vegetarian mince
  • 1 medium red onion, finely diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 bell pepper, finely diced
  • 2 jalapeño peppers, finely diced and deseeded for less heat
  • 1 medium head green cabbage, cored and roughly chopped
  • 5 ml olive oil
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/2 tsp onion powder
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper, optional
  • 150 ml enchilada sauce
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • 100 g finely shredded cheese
  • 150 g Greek yogurt, for serving
  • Fresh coriander, for serving
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges

Directions

  1. Dice the onion, garlic, bell pepper and jalapeños. Core and roughly chop the cabbage.
  2. Heat olive oil in a large deep skillet over medium heat. Cook onion and garlic for 2–3 minutes.
  3. Add veggie mince, smoked paprika, garlic powder, cumin, oregano, onion powder, optional cayenne and black pepper. Cook according to the mince package directions, breaking it into small pieces.
  4. Add bell pepper and jalapeños. Pour in enchilada sauce, add cabbage and cover.
  5. Cook covered for 10–12 minutes. Stir, then cook uncovered another 4–5 minutes until excess liquid reduces.
  6. Top with shredded cheese, cover and cook about 2 minutes until melted.
  7. Serve with Greek yogurt, fresh coriander and lime juice.

Note: Using individual spices instead of a taco-seasoning packet gives you more control over sodium. The veggie mince, enchilada sauce and cheese may already contain plenty of salt, so taste before adding any.
