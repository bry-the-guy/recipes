Creamy Broccoli, Feta & White Bean Soup

4 servings · about 55 minutes

A creamy, high-protein broccoli soup with feta, cannellini beans and cottage cheese, adjusted to keep the sodium lower without losing the thick, velvety texture.

Ingredients

  • 450 g broccoli florets, chopped fairly small
  • 60 g baby spinach
  • 1 small yellow onion, quartered
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 140 g feta cheese
  • 15 ml olive oil
  • 720 ml low-sodium or unsalted chicken or bone broth
  • 110 g cottage cheese
  • 1 can cannellini beans, about 240 g drained, thoroughly rinsed
  • Zest and juice of ½ lemon
  • 1 tbsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp chili flakes
  • Black pepper, to taste

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 200°C conventional or 180°C fan.
  2. Add the broccoli, onion, garlic and feta to a large baking dish or sheet pan. Drizzle with olive oil and season with oregano, chili flakes and black pepper. Do not add salt yet.
  3. Roast for 35–40 minutes, stirring the broccoli once halfway through, until tender with browned edges.
  4. Transfer the roasted broccoli, onion, garlic and feta to a blender. Add the cottage cheese, cannellini beans, spinach, lemon zest, lemon juice and 650 ml of the broth.
  5. Blend for 60–90 seconds until completely smooth and creamy. Add the remaining broth gradually until it reaches your preferred consistency.
  6. Pour into a saucepan and warm gently for 5–10 minutes. Taste before adding any salt. Adjust with extra lemon, black pepper or chili flakes if needed.
  7. Serve warm. Optional toppings: fresh dill, extra chili flakes, or a small amount of finely grated Parmesan or Manchego.
Lower-sodium notes: Use low-sodium or unsalted broth, rinse the beans thoroughly, and taste before adding any salt. The feta and cottage cheese already add saltiness.
