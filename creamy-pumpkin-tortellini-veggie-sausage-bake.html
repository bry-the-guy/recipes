Creamy Pumpkin Tortellini & Veggie Sausage Bake

Photo by Ivan Mudruk via Pexels.

6 servings · about 45 minutes

An easy creamy pumpkin tortellini bake with vegetarian sausage and broccoli.

Ingredients

  • 565 g refrigerated cheese tortellini
  • 340 g fully cooked vegetarian sausage, sliced
  • 425 g Alfredo sauce
  • 120 g pumpkin purée
  • 120 ml low-sodium vegetable broth
  • 200 g broccoli florets, cut into small bite-size pieces
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • ½ tsp Italian seasoning
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • ¼ tsp red pepper flakes, optional
  • Pinch of ground nutmeg
  • 110 g shredded mozzarella
  • 25 g grated Parmesan

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 200°C conventional / 180°C fan. Lightly grease a roughly 23 × 33 cm baking dish.
  2. Stir the Alfredo, pumpkin, vegetable broth, garlic powder, onion powder, Italian seasoning, black pepper, optional red pepper flakes, and nutmeg directly in the dish until smooth.
  3. Add the tortellini, sliced vegetarian sausage, and broccoli. Stir until evenly coated.
  4. Cover tightly with foil and bake for 20 minutes.
  5. Remove the foil and stir. Top with mozzarella and Parmesan.
  6. Bake uncovered for another 8–10 minutes, until bubbly and golden in spots.
  7. Rest for 5 minutes before serving so the sauce thickens.
