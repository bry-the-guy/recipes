Pesto White Bean Pasta Bake

Photo by Davis Arenas via Pexels.

6 servings · about 50 minutes

Ingredients

  • 340 g dry farfalle pasta
  • 600 ml low-sodium vegetable broth
  • 190 g pesto
  • 1 can white beans, about 240 g drained and rinsed
  • 60 g baby spinach, roughly chopped
  • 100 g shredded mozzarella
  • 100 g finely grated Parmesan

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 190°C conventional / 170°C fan.
  2. Add dry pasta and rinsed white beans to a 23 × 33 cm baking dish.
  3. Whisk the vegetable broth and pesto together. Stir in most of the mozzarella and Parmesan, reserving a little of each for the top.
  4. Scatter the chopped spinach over the pasta and beans. Pour the pesto-broth mixture evenly over the dish and gently stir so the pasta is coated and mostly submerged.
  5. Top with the reserved cheese and cover tightly with foil. Bake for 35 minutes.
  6. Remove foil and bake another 5 minutes. Rest for 5 minutes, then gently stir before serving.
